Privacy policy

Punchable · last updated October 26, 2026

Punchable is a personal joke-writing app made and run by Tristan Rega for his own stand-up material. Sign-up is closed: only the owner's account can sign in.

What Punchable stores

  • Your account: your name and email address from Google sign-in.
  • Your material: jokes, notes, set recordings' transcripts and laugh measurements, boards and the edits you make in the app.

Google data

With your permission, Punchable connects to your Google Drive to keep your joke documents and Punchable in sync. It reads your Google Docs to bring new or edited jokes into the app, and it creates and updates documents only in its own "Punchable" folder. It never writes to your other documents, and it skips legal documents entirely.

Punchable's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the sync feature you turned on. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept

Your material is stored on a server operated by the owner and on the owner's own computer. Text you choose to work on with the app's writing tools may be processed by the AI service the owner uses for that. Audio is processed on the owner's own hardware.

Your choices

  • You can disconnect Google at any time from your Google Account's security settings (third-party access).
  • You can export your material from Settings, and ask for it to be deleted by emailing the address below.

Questions or deletion requests: technicallyinclined@proton.me
