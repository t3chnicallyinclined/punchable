Pick Punchable's logo

Three concepts, each shown the way it will actually appear: the app icon from favicon size up, the mark on its own, and next to the name. Tell me A, B or C, or what to change.

A · Mic P Recommended

A stage mic drawn as a P. The three slots on the bowl are the mic grille.

Why it's the pick: it's the initial and the tool of the trade in one shape. It still reads as a P and a mic in a browser tab, and it works in one colour on a shirt or a flyer.

B · Punch line

The letter P with an amber underline: the punch line, underlined.

Trade-off: the simplest of the three and very clean, but at tab size it's just a P. It reads as "a P app" more than a comedy tool.

C · Laugh meter

The app's own 4-bar laugh meter: chuckle, laugh, big laugh, applause break.

Trade-off: it's literally what the app measures, and it's very legible small. It also looks like a phone's signal-strength icon, so it can get lost on a home screen.

The lockups here set the name in the app's display font. The final versions in the repo have it drawn as outlines. Every concept has dark, light and one-colour versions in punchable/docs/brand/. Concept A is already wired in as the app icon, favicon and splash; switching to B or C is a one-line change.
